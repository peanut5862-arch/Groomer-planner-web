Privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Hey Betty is a planner for grooming businesses. This policy describes how the Hey Betty service handles account information and the business information you enter. For support or privacy requests, email HeyBetty.support@gmail.com.

Information used by the service

We process account email addresses and authentication information; business settings and team invitations; client names, contact information and addresses; pet details and service notes; appointments, confirmation status, prices, payment-method records, tips and groomer commissions. Payment records document payments; they are not a request to enter bank passwords or full payment-card numbers.

When you use Ask Betty, your questions, recent conversation and relevant planner information are processed to provide an answer. When you request location-based travel estimates and allow device location access, your current location is used for that request. Saved starting and client addresses are used for routing. The service also uses technical information, such as connection information and error logs, to operate and troubleshoot the app.

How information is used

Information supports sign-in, account recovery, business setup, scheduling, routing, payment records, team access, invitations, support and Ask Betty. Business owners manage their team’s access. Groomer accounts have limited access to assigned work; owners should invite only people authorized to view that information.

Service providers and sharing

Hey Betty uses Supabase for accounts and business data, Vercel for website and server hosting, Resend for account and invitation emails, Google Maps services for routing, and OpenAI for Ask Betty. Relevant information is sent to these providers to perform those functions. For example, routing can involve addresses or requested device location, and Ask Betty can involve your question and relevant client or schedule information.

Information may also be shared with authorized members of your business, in response to applicable legal requirements, or when needed to investigate misuse and protect the service. Opening external maps, email, phone or messaging apps subjects those interactions to their own providers’ policies.

Your choices

You can edit business and client information through the app where available, manage team invitations and access, and control location and notification permissions in your device settings. Ask Betty’s current conversation clears when you close it using X; that does not guarantee immediate deletion of provider logs or backups.

To request account deletion, access or correction of information, contact support from your account email. We may need to verify your identity and authority over the business. If you are a grooming customer whose information was entered by a business using Hey Betty, contact that business first; support can also help direct your request.

Storage, retention and security

The service uses cloud storage and local device/browser storage for sessions and app operation. We retain information as needed to provide the service, support accounts, resolve issues and meet applicable obligations. Deletion may take additional time in backups and provider systems. Provider processing may occur outside your state or country. We use access controls and authenticated connections; no system can guarantee complete security.

Business responsibilities

Businesses should have permission to enter and use their clients’ and team members’ information, keep access limited to authorized people, and avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information in notes or AI questions. Hey Betty is intended for business use, not for children under 13.

Policy updates and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when practices change. For questions, privacy requests or technical help, contact HeyBetty.support@gmail.com. Please describe the issue and your device or browser; do not email passwords, reset links or payment-card details.
